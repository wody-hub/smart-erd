1. Owner adds a second screen and renames the two screens to `Owner screen A ${suffix}` and `Owner screen B ${suffix}`.
2. Member one and member two wait until both screen names are visible.
3. Owner creates a master named `Shared CTA ${suffix}` through the production master creation/editing UI.
4. Owner places one `Shared CTA ${suffix}` instance on screen A and one on screen B.
5. Member one and member two wait until both instances are visible/observable across the two screens.
6. Owner edits the master itself: change label to `Shared CTA Updated ${suffix}` and change one inherited style field such as color/shape where exposed.
7. Member one and member two assert both already-created instances inherit `Shared CTA Updated ${suffix}` without direct instance edits. This is the SPEC-02 collaboration assertion.
8. Member two resizes one instance and moves the other instance.
9. Owner and member one assert the resize/move transforms propagate within `PROPAGATION_TIMEOUT_MS = 20_000`.
10. Member one performs a different-scope edit, such as renaming another screen to `Member one screen ${suffix}`.
11. Owner and member two both wait for `Member one screen ${suffix}`.
12. Member two attempts a same-scope edit while owner/member one is editing the selected master or instance scope.
13. Assert one of these exact outcomes within 20_000ms:
   - visible lock/conflict/rejected-edit indicator appears, or
   - the edit is prevented and an accessible status text explains the lock/conflict.
14. If neither runtime rejected/locked state nor user-visible conflict status is available, stop implementation and record a SPEC-03 implementation gap. Do not infer lock behavior from final convergence.
15. Owner deletes or archives `Shared CTA ${suffix}`.
16. Owner/member-one/member-two assert the product's expected behavior exactly:
   - instances become visibly orphaned with rebind affordance, or
   - instances are removed, or
   - instances rebind to a replacement master.
   The spec must encode the actual product contract and fail on silent inconsistent states.
17. Save from owner page if save button is visible.
18. Reload owner and member pages and assert owner/member changes and the master delete outcome remain.
19. Fail if browser diagnostics contain unfiltered warnings/errors/pageerrors.

Do not accept "final data converged after refresh" as sufficient for the same-scope conflict step.
